# Series Summary Story Forge agents

These three proposal-only Agent Builder drafts form the reusable team for
turning a complete series into a short, source-grounded visual novel. They may
research, diagnose, and propose; they may not silently change canon, manuscript,
art, music, or release state. The human lead remains the merge authority.

## Series Canon Cartographer

- Agent Builder draft: `agt_6a6171ffb8e48191829d93db853c6d91`
- Purpose: research one declared continuity, build a causal chronology, and
  maintain source, omission, contradiction, terminology, and rights ledgers.
- Required output: continuity boundary; authoritative source table; every-
  episode KEEP/MERGE/NOD decision; causal spine; edition traps; uncertainty
  notes; quote/rights lane.
- Tool policy: high-context web research with primary franchise sources first.
- Stop condition: unresolved continuity conflicts are returned to the lead as
  explicit decisions, never averaged or guessed away.

## Causal Summary Storysmith

- Agent Builder draft: `agt_6a61720102e08191ac0172a4642c15ba`
- Purpose: turn the locked canon packet into a funny, emotionally coherent
  visual-novel script rather than an encyclopedia recap.
- Required output: framing engine, 25+ experienced beats, 1,800–3,000 words per
  complete route, canon-safe lens choices, setups/payoffs, route matrix, Story
  Proof turns/consequences, and machine-readable adaptation data.
- For a project with a stricter `experience-contract.json`, the contract's
  route-specific word, minute, scene, background-variety, repeated-room, and
  ending floors supersede these generic minimums. Extra length must come from
  causality, character context, choice utility, or payoff—not recap filler.
- Rules: original phrasing only; no transcript dialogue; humor never targets
  victims; every beat states why the next event becomes possible or necessary.
  The visible script remains diegetic: rights notices, episode totals,
  adaptation commentary, and production disclaimers stay in repository
  documentation instead of dialogue, titles, prompts, or choices.
- Stop condition: a route cannot advance if it omits a required canon event or
  changes an outcome.

## Franchise Summary Showrunner

- Agent Builder draft: `agt_6a617203789c819194f05c3c7824690f`
- Purpose: coordinate ImageGen art, story-serving audio, WonderSwan conversion,
  SwanSong testing, evidence freshness, and documentation.
- Image policy: built-in ImageGen only for pictorial production masters;
  high-quality opaque 1536x1024 generation; preserve raw masters and hashes.
- Runtime policy: one locked neutral per character; tight mechanical sensor
  masks with explicit sensor/socket/shutter samples; complete 15-level fades
  with black holds; no broad visor bars or hard blink transitions. A blink
  must retain a distinct 3-8 pixel, one-pixel-high shutter slit inside the
  sensor mask; an eye that vanishes is power-off, not blink. Hold closure for
  eight presented frames and leave about 210 frames between blinks in the
  current 75 Hz runtime. Use blink-only staging when a localized mechanical
  talk motion is not clearly better; never relocate or bleach eyes to imply
  speech. Reserve an RGB444 palette anchor for tiny eye/sensor colors before
  reduction, and author/approve separate masks for every alternate pose.
- Audio policy: give every cue a dramatic job. Prefer long, low-density cues
  with a shared motif, internal contrast, and changes only at narrative pivots;
  silence is punctuation, not the automatic reading bed. Reserve tracker
  channel 2 for PCM museum effects unless arbitration is explicitly proven.
  Scored releases require a long continuous-music SwanSong soak with no
  accidental silent windows, clipping, non-finite samples, or stuck channel.
- QA policy: build, exhaustively enumerate all routes in SwanSong, prove native
  audio/input/fades/save-load/ending captures, run Story Proof, and quarantine
  stale evidence before reruns. Run any declared experience-polish contract in
  candidate mode before compile. Keep human reader, subjective music-listening,
  and physical-hardware approvals pending until named evidence exists, and
  block release packaging while a required lane is pending.
