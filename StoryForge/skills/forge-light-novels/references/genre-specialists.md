# Genre Specialist Modules

Genre modules are lenses, not formulas. They identify missing delivery evidence
and ask sharper questions; the lead writer decides how the book answers them.

## Mystery

- Keep a clue ledger with plant scene, reader-visible evidence, plausible wrong
  inference, payoff scene, and changed meaning.
- Make at least one competing theory fair before the solution.
- Do not count withheld information as a clue.
- The solution should force a consequential choice, not merely name a culprit.

## Romance

- Ground attraction in observed, specific behavior.
- Record boundaries, permission, or consent as visible choices when relevant.
- Let the core obstacle test values or needs; avoid solvability by one omitted
  sentence alone.
- Require reciprocal changed behavior for the emotional payoff.

## Comedy and Cozy Fiction

- Escalate inconvenience through established character logic.
- Track the recurring ritual, place, or task that becomes more welcoming.
- Vary joke mechanics: status, specificity, reversal, timing, callback, and
  physical constraint.
- Protect at least one sincere landing from an immediate joke cancellation.

## Adventure

- Maintain a concrete objective with visible progress and setbacks.
- Change obstacle tactics, information, cost, and relationships rather than
  only scale.
- Show competence through a specific learned action and its limitation.
- Make the climax demand a value-revealing risk, sacrifice, or commitment.

## Other Profiles

Use the manifest's required pleasures and module checks. Record planned
delivery and payoff scenes, then judge the result in prose and with readers.
Never average genre satisfaction into a number that conceals a missing core
pleasure.
