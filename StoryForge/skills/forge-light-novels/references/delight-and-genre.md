## Delight is specific

Do not equate fun with joke count. Delight can be humor, surprise, tenderness,
wonder, competence, recognition, catharsis, or a satisfying reversal. Record
why a moment could happen only in this story with these characters.

## Signature moments

Give every chapter at least one `delight.signature_moments` entry. It must name
the scene, delight type, setup, delivery, reader effect, and `only_here_reason`.
The reason cannot merely repeat the premise. Prefer a tell-someone-else moment:
an image, choice, exchange, or reversal a reader can describe without retelling
the whole plot.

## Emotional rhythm

Record tension, warmth, humor, and wonder from 0-5 for every scene. The numbers
are planning coordinates, not quality scores. Use them to expose long flat runs,
unprepared tonal jumps, and chapters that open or close without pull. Every
rhythm entry also records its dominant beat, intended reader effect, entry hook,
and exit pull.

Quiet sequences may be intentionally level. Document a narrow waiver and the
relationship or thematic change that occurs inside the quiet run.

## Character chemistry

For each important relationship, define:

- `status_game`: who appears to hold power and how it can flip;
- `friction`: the recurring incompatibility that creates motion;
- `shared_joke`: a comic pattern whose meaning may evolve;
- `secret_tenderness`: care shown before it can be spoken;
- `conversation_game`: the tactics each person uses in dialogue;
- `status_flips`: scene-specific changes in permission or power.

The chemistry pass checks action, timing, and subtext. It does not accept a list
of affectionate adjectives as evidence.

## Genre profiles

Choose a built-in profile from `assets/genre-profiles.json` or use `custom`.
Built-in pleasures are reader promises, not mandatory plot beats. Each required
pleasure gets a planned delivery and payoff scene. Add a freshness move and
forbidden shortcuts so satisfying the genre does not become imitation.

For a custom profile, define at least three expectations and corresponding
module checks. Hybrid books use one primary module and record secondary
expectations without forcing two complete formulas onto the same volume.

## Reader enjoyment evidence

Use at least one unprimed general reader and one intended-audience reader.
Optionally add a genre enthusiast. In addition to comprehension questions, ask:

- Where did you smile, laugh, feel surprise, or feel moved?
- Where did you skim or want the scene to end?
- What line or image would you quote?
- What would you tell someone else about this book?
- Did you want the next chapter or volume? Why?

Record disagreement. Do not revise toward an average reader who does not exist.

## Music as a delight system

A soundtrack is optional. When it strengthens the project, give characters,
places, relationships, comedy, mystery, or emotional turns memorable motifs.
State how each motif changes when status, permission, or meaning changes. Favor
one clear, playful hook over busy accompaniment.

For WonderSwan adaptation, plan BPM, meter, tonal center, loop bars, channel 1
lead, channel 2 bass, channel 3 harmony/sweep, channel 4 percussion/noise, mono
safety, and one hardware-specific feature. Listen for loop seams, channel
crowding, clipping, fatigue, and whether the cue is actually fun. A structural
report cannot replace listening.
