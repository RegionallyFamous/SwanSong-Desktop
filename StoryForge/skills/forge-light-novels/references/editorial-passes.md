# Editorial Passes

## Evidence rule

Separate diagnosis from revision. For every pass, cite scene IDs, explain the
reader effect, record what changed, and verify the new draft. “Flows well” and
“polished” are not evidence. Do not perform every pass simultaneously: line
editing can beautify a scene that should be removed.

## Required passes

### Developmental

Test the reader promise, genre pleasures, middle escalation, stakes, chapter
turns, and ending. Ask whether removing or combining a scene improves causality.

### Scene causality

Trace every `because_of` link against the manuscript. Reject scenes that exist
only to add information, length, or travel between sets.

### Character voice

Compare diction, syntax, evasions, metaphors, tactics, and behavior under
pressure. Review the voice fingerprint report, then read dialogue without names.
Automation may expose similarity; the editor decides whether it is a problem.

### Comedy and tone

Map setup, escalation, reversal, callback, and sincere landing. Remove joke
echoes that flatten vulnerability. Confirm tonal shifts are prepared.

### Chemistry and delight

Trace status, permission, intimacy, friction, shared jokes, and secret tenderness
through behavior. Verify signature moments are prepared and land in the draft.
Ask whether readers receive a specific pattern of laughter, surprise, tenderness,
wonder, competence, recognition, or catharsis. Do not turn the map into a joke
quota or sand down surprise to satisfy a uniform rhythm.

### Continuity and payoff

Audit objects, time, knowledge, promises, setup IDs, payoff IDs, motif evolution,
series canon, and illustration continuity. A callback must change meaning.

### Line and prose

Review the prose report, then remove abstraction, redundant explanation, generic
sensory filler, filter phrases, rhythm monotony, and accidental repetition.
Preserve deliberate voice irregularities.

### Read aloud

Read the exact final draft aloud or use a faithful read-aloud pass. Record
stumbles, breathless sentences, ambiguous speakers, dead jokes, and emotional
beats interrupted by explanation.

### Publication polish

Review title hierarchy, scene breaks, page rhythm, front/back matter, metadata,
cover copy, image placement, captions, alt text, orphan/widow behavior, and
rendered all-page proofs and contact sheet. Confirm EPUB and PDF feel like the same edition
and every image earns its interruption of prose.

## Analysis reports

Run character voice, prose polish, chapter momentum, scene delivery, continuity,
reader synthesis, and rights tools against the exact draft. Add the soundtrack
report when music is enabled. Warnings are revision leads, not automatic deletions. A similar
voice may be intentional family resemblance; an adverb may carry comic timing;
a flat passage may be deliberate stillness. Record what the editor inspected,
what changed or stayed, and why. Bind report contents and reviewer response to
the manuscript SHA-256.

## Revision ledger

Use severities `critical`, `major`, `minor`, or `note`. Mark an item `resolved`
only after checking the revised manuscript. Release blocks unresolved critical
items. Each entry answers:

- Where is the problem?
- What reader experience does it damage?
- What changed?
- How was the change verified?

## Reader enjoyment protocol

Use at least one unprimed general reader and one unprimed intended-audience
reader who did not produce the draft. Add a genre specialist when genre fluency
is central. Ask:

1. What moment stayed with you?
2. Where did attention drift or meaning become unclear?
3. What did you expect at the midpoint?
4. What did the protagonist want?
5. What feeling remained after the final line?
6. Where did you smile, laugh, feel surprise, or feel moved?
7. Where did you skim or want the scene to end?
8. What line or image would you keep?
9. How would you describe the book to someone else?
10. Did you want the next chapter or volume, and why?

Record responses accurately. The author may decline a suggestion but must record
the reason and revision response.

## Release approval

An AI may assemble reports, highlight evidence, and suggest a decision. It may
not invent a human editor, reader response, art approval, or release approval.
Set approval to `approved` only when a responsible person has reviewed the exact
final draft, publication configuration, and approved ImageGen asset set. Any
prose or asset-byte change invalidates its stored hash and requires review.

## Scene-delivery and reader-synthesis passes

For each scene, compare drafted evidence with the planned turn, decision,
consequence, chemistry move, signature moment, and exit pull. Record
`delivered`, `revised`, or `waived` for every dimension and cite a concrete
manuscript quote. A waiver explains why relocating or removing the promise makes
the book stronger. Judge dramatic effect, not keyword overlap.

Keep individual reader reports intact. A separate accountable editor records
consensus, meaningful disagreement, target/genre expectation results, confusion
patterns, delight patterns, revision decisions, and material intentionally left
unchanged. Never collapse this evidence into average taste. Bind the synthesis
to the exact manuscript hash.
