# Human reader/playtest approval packet

Status: **pending**

This is a real-person approval lane. Automated SwanSong playthroughs prove that
routes advance and terminate; they do not prove that a newcomer understands
the history or that the tour feels satisfying.

Test at least three readers:

1. one person unfamiliar with the original story;
2. one person who remembers its broad plot;
3. one person who knows it closely.

Each reader should complete one route without coaching, then answer:

- Who made the three choices that most changed what happened next?
- Why did White Base become more than a weapons carrier?
- What did the Newtype material mean beyond improved combat?
- Which exhibit was confusing, emotionally effective, or unexpectedly funny?
- Did the optional plaque feel useful or like homework?
- Did the ending feel complete after the final input?
- Where did attention drag, and where did the tour move too quickly?
- Which image, sound, or phrase remained memorable ten minutes later?

Record the exact ROM SHA-256, route choices, session length, reader background,
answers, observed hesitation, and revision decision. Approval requires all
three sessions, a written synthesis, and named evidence files. Do not change
this packet's status merely because automated route coverage passes.
