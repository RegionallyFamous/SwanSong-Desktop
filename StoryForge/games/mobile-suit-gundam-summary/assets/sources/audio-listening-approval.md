# Human music-listening approval packet

Status: **pending**

Automated checks cover cue wiring, loop length, finite samples, reserved PCM
capacity, and transport stability. A person must still judge whether the score
is musical, emotionally appropriate, and comfortable during extended reading.

Listen to every cue for two complete loops on headphones and ordinary laptop
speakers, then play one full people route and one full power route in SwanSong.
For each cue record:

- whether the melody is memorable without becoming intrusive;
- whether the bass and pulse remain balanced under museum SFX;
- whether the loop seam is perceptible;
- whether any register becomes tiring after two minutes;
- whether the cue's dramatic job matches its scenes;
- whether a transition feels like a cut, restart, or emotional contradiction.

The two ending cues must read as related transformations with different
conclusions. Approval requires a named listener, date, exact project and ROM
hashes, completed notes for all seven cues, and explicit accept/revise
decisions. Physical-speaker character belongs to the separate hardware lane.
