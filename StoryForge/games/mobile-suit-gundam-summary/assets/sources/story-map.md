# Story map — Last Tour Before Closing

The playable script is `game_spec.json`; this page is its review map.

## Promise

Two living SD mobile-suit docents have twenty minutes before the museum closes
to guide the final visitors across one year of war. They promise causality
instead of a list of cool fights. The closing clock, blank tour ticket, broken
exit arrow, tangled Zabi board, and HERO/VILLAIN badges are visible setups.

## Route shape

```text
War / Side 7 / White Base escape
              |
      choose Earth gallery
       /              \
White Base         war map
       \              /
 Ral / Odessa / Miharu / Jaburo / Side 6
              |
      choose final archive
       /              \
    people          power
       \              /
Solomon / Texas / Lalah / Solar Ray / A Baoa Qu / home
```

Choices change the language of attention, not facts or outcomes. The four
experienced routes contain 1,853–1,867 words across at least 33 runtime nodes.

## Payoff

Amuro begins alone inside a weapon and ends by guiding, and being guided by,
the White Base crew. The museum's broken exit arrow resolves into the escape
path; the ticket's last punch is HOME; HERO and VILLAIN become WITNESS. The
final image is not a faction color but warm light leading people out together.

## Tone rules

- Joke about museum bureaucracy, rushed docents, overfull footnote carts, tiny
  bodies, and bad display labels.
- Never joke about civilian deaths, refugees, grief, or rank-and-file soldiers.
- Let every comic beat either clarify a causal connection or create breathing
  room before the next serious turn.
