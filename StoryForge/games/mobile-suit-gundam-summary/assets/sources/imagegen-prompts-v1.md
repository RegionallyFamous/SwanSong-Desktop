# ImageGen production record — v1

All eight production masters were created with the built-in ImageGen tool.
The raw outputs are preserved unchanged; cutouts and runtime reductions are
derived assets. No procedural or code-painted pictorial fallback was used.

## Docent RX-78-2

> Full-body faithful super-deformed RX-78-2 Gundam as a living little robot
> museum docent, one character only, fully mechanical anatomy, canonical white
> armor with blue torso, red feet and chin, yellow vents and V-fin, oversized
> SD head and compact body, friendly open-hand explaining pose, other hand at
> hip, crisp cel animation rendering with clean dark outlines and readable
> mechanical panels, centered with complete feet and V-fin, designed to remain
> legible at 96x128 pixels. Perfectly flat solid chroma-key magenta #ff00ff
> background. No human skin, no human eyes, no costume, no weapon, no text, no
