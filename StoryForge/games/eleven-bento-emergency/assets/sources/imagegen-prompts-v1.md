Each module is a simple angular white/navy/red armor plate from Aerial's shield system. At far right one living SD burgundy Darilbalde walks away while the lowest right-hand module carries pickles and follows.
Mandatory object count: five modules left + six modules right = eleven total. Do not draw a twelfth module.
Mandatory stave design: plain angular shield armor pieces only. No stave may have a head, face, eye, V-fin, arms, legs or humanoid body.
World rule: Aerial and Darilbalde are living little robot neighbors; the eleven Bit Staves are nonliving equipment
Style/medium: polished retro 1990s Japanese cel-animation game illustration, crisp 16-bit handheld readability, bold dark outlines, simple cel shadows, sparse mechanical detail
Composition/framing: Aerial centered; five modules clearly separated on left, six clearly separated on right; Darilbalde secondary; quiet open upper-left title field; calm low-detail lower strip; all silhouettes inside frame
Critical face rule: Aerial and Darilbalde have mechanical helmets and camera eyes only, never human facial anatomy
Constraints: exactly one Aerial, one Darilbalde, eleven staves; no other robots; no text, letters, numbers, logos, decals, unit markings, watermark, pseudo-writing or random symbols; no combat, weapons in use, beam effects, pilots, hangar, cockpit, model-kit tools, boxes, stands or toy photography
