# ImageGen Prompts v1

Tool mode: built-in ImageGen. Each asset was generated in a separate call.

## `background_title_imagegen_v1.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: high-resolution title-background source master for a WonderSwan Color visual novel, designed for a wide 14:9 crop and final 224x144 downsample
Primary request: Create the title key art for a cozy comedy called The GM Name-Tag Crisis. Four living super-deformed classic RGM-79 GM mobile suits are community-fair volunteers at a check-in table. They are deliberately identical: white and red armor, simple green visor, blocky mechanical faces, compact backpacks, broad feet. Each holds the same blank name badge; beside them are four matching tote bags, four matching umbrellas, and four matching blue star stickers. A small round Ball-type robot coordinator looks baffled. The humor must read only through posture and camera-eye direction.
World rule: These are living little robot people doing ordinary neighborhood activities, never toys or model kits.
Style/medium: polished retro 1990s Japanese cel-animation game illustration with crisp intentional 16-bit handheld readability, bold dark colored outlines, sparse purposeful mechanical detail, simple cel shadows, human-designed composition
Composition/framing: wide ensemble, all heads and antennae safely inside frame, one clear focal cluster, quiet open upper-left field for title UI, calm low-detail lower strip for dialogue UI, simple fair hall background
Color palette: warm cream hall, classic GM white/red/green, muted teal and sunflower accents; four to six dominant colors plus accents
Critical face rule: every mobile suit has a fully mechanical helmet, visor, faceplate and chin guard; absolutely no human mouths, noses, cheeks, eyebrows, eyelashes, skin or anime eyes
Constraints: exact classic GM silhouettes; duplicates are intentional; no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no decals, no unit markings, no watermark; no weapons, combat, pilots, hangar, cockpit, model-kit tools, sprues, boxes or stands
Avoid: pseudo-text, random symbols, clutter, extra limbs, fused hands, repeated bags beyond the four specified, photorealism, toy photography, 3D plastic model appearance
```
