Composition/framing: wide scene, Virtue and attendant as one clear focal interaction, detached armor stack as secondary prop cluster, quiet open upper-left title field, calm low-detail lower strip for dialogue UI, all antennae and panels safely inside frame
Color palette: Virtue white/charcoal/green/red, warm amber lobby, muted teal coat counter, scarf accents; four to six dominant colors
