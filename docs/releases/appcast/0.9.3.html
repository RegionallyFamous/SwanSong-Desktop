Keep long translation proof moving.

SwanSong 0.9.3 closes two runtime gaps in long-form translation verification without exposing private game material.

  • Keep the end of the route. Original and Patched captures retain their final 30 emulated frames of audio during the same replay.
  • Carry the exact save forward. Seal one authenticated Patched capture into separate private LOAD and CONTINUE clones.
  • Check both paths cleanly. Each consumer starts from clean power and retains its own final frame and audio window.
  • Keep receipts source-safe. Public results contain bindings and counts, never game text, save bytes, screenshots, audio, or private paths.

SwanSong 0.9.3 is the current stable release. It is offered on the default update channel.
