A clearer welcome to your WonderSwan library.

SwanSong 0.9.1 adds a calm first-run guide and clearer language throughout the app.

  • Start in three steps. Add a game, press Play, then save or rewind whenever you like.
  • Find help again. Reopen Start Here and What’s New from the Help menu.
  • Know what will happen. Rewind, save-state, translation, and recovery actions now explain their results more clearly.
  • Keep trust precise. Translation Shelf distinguishes package verification from trust in its source.

SwanSong 0.9.1 is the current stable release. It is offered on the default update channel.

SwanSong includes no games, translations, or original system firmware. Read the full release notes.
